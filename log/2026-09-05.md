Two releases of the Story Planner and Timeline overhaul today, v2.43.0 and v2.44.0.

The first gate was mostly about admitting a feature hadn't worked. The Snowflake Wizard was meant to
walk you from a one-line premise up to a full outline, and in practice nobody finished it — you'd get
three steps in and abandon it. I pulled it out and replaced it with eleven starter Jots you can pick
up in any order, or ignore entirely.

Two follow-ups from that, both small and both my own fault:

- The starter Jots inherited copy from the wizard that promised automation they don't do. Writing a
  cheque the feature can't cash is worse than saying nothing.
- "Add Starter Jots" was confirming through a dialog whose primary button was a red *Delete*. It had
  been that way for a while, which is the part that bothers me — I'd looked straight past it more
  than once.

The second gate was the interesting one. Plot threads can now branch and merge, which meant the
timeline could no longer be drawn as a set of parallel lines. Redrew it as a proper thread chart
where a branch visibly leaves its parent and a merge visibly rejoins.

That surfaced a real bug rather than a cosmetic one. `resolveThreadTree` could be handed a cycle —
threads that eventually loop back into each other — and the cycle-breaking picked an essentially
arbitrary edge to cut, so the same project could render a different shape between runs. Now it
demotes the thread that closes the ring specifically, which is both stable and the answer a reader
would expect.

Non-determinism in a rendering path is the kind of bug that wastes a whole afternoon later,
because you can't reproduce it on demand. Worth the detour.
