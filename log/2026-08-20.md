Two commits, two releases.

v2.20.0 added a tap-to-pick calendar and clock, plus relative scene dates — so a scene can be "three
days after the last one" instead of a date you work out yourself.

v2.21.0 let entity references link by name rather than by pasted identifier. I had been copying UUIDs
between fields, which worked and was absurd.

Both are the same complaint: the app knew the answer and was making me supply it anyway.
