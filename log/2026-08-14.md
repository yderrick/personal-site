Twenty commits and six phases turning a desktop app into something usable on a touchscreen, ending at
v2.0.0.

Worked through it in stages rather than all at once: hygiene and build first, then the PWA shell,
breakpoint hooks and layout tokens, a proper mobile shell, then the straightforward workspaces. The
hard part was last — the canvases. Relation Graph, Temporal Timeline, Story Planner and the
manuscript editor chrome each needed their own answer for touch.

Dragging was its own problem. Wrote a pointer-based drag hook, proved it on Jots alone, then rolled
it out to the remaining four drag sites once I trusted it.

Three fixes worth remembering:

- Modals opened from a mobile pane swallowed every tap. Shared `<Modal>` backdrop fixed it.
- Timeline drops landed years later than where they were released — the drop maths assumed a
  coordinate space that touch didn't use.
- The plot-timeline curve was measured after a 50ms guess. Replaced with a ResizeObserver, which is
  what I should have written the first time. A timeout that usually works is a bug waiting for a
  slower device.
