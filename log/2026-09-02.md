Twelve commits, released as v2.31.0.

Reworked the Chekhov Ledger, which pairs the things you plant with the things you pay off. It had
been guessing at pairings; now a payoff can name the exact plant it settles, or pick from a list, and
explicit links are reported as explicit rather than inferred. It also returns entries in narrative
order instead of the order the analysis pass happened to visit them — the pass order meant nothing to
a reader.

Redrew the Lie-to-Need arc as a real SVG line chart. It had been distorting its own dots and axis
labels, which is a specific kind of bad: a chart that is wrong in a way that still looks like a
chart.

Two smaller fixes with the same shape. The Continuity Audit now grades findings on whether scenes
carry a time of day, rather than treating a missing time as a contradiction. And when several
same-day pairings conflict, it grades the most severe rather than the first one it found — first-found
is an implementation detail leaking into a judgement.

Added a one-click carry-over of the previous scene's cast, surfaced outside the tag accordion where I
would actually see it, and made Deaths in this Scene collapsible.
