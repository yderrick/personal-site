Three releases in a day, v2.22.0 through v2.24.0.

The substantive one: eight fields that used to hold free text now link to entities. "Mentor" was a
string containing a character's name; it's now a reference to that character. Which means renaming
the character updates it, and you can ask who mentors whom.

Also gave the Lore Explorer a layout that uses the desktop screen instead of a narrow column on a
wide monitor, and made the Reference Panel keep the same shape rather than reflowing as you move
between entity types. A panel that rearranges itself teaches you nothing about where to look.

One fix: the storyboard's opening field was labelled Goal when what it actually holds is a
description. A wrong label is worse than a missing one — people fill it in correctly for the label
they read.
