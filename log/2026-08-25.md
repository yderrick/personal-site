Nine commits, two releases, v2.28.0 and v2.29.0.

The whole day was one theme: the app was throwing away work. Switching tabs or chapters discarded
pending manuscript edits. Leaving the Lore Explorer with unsaved changes lost them silently.

Fixed both. Added a dirty-tracking layer to the lore form with an "Unsaved changes" pill, and made
leaving park the edits rather than drop them — you get them back when you return, instead of a dialog
asking whether you meant it. Cross-workspace history plumbing so navigation between workspaces is a
real route with real entries.

Losing someone's writing is the worst thing a writing app can do. Everything else is an
inconvenience; that one makes you stop trusting it, and you don't come back.

Also fixed the Reference Panel's Physical Description and Voice fields and its prose truncation,
added an Edit-in-Lore jump from the panel, and put the Writing Scene Guide on the mobile keyboard
toolbar.
