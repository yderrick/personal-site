Spent the day surfacing data the app already had but wasn't showing.

Every Scene Card field now appears in the Writing Scene Guide, with a minimise toggle so it can get
out of the way. Added Connector, Scene Charge and Cliffhanger Type to the Storyboard scene form, then
carried those through to the scene export and the writing guide so the same information exists
everywhere you'd look for it.

Also added a collapsible Spine sidebar to the Manuscript workspace.

The pattern I keep hitting: a field that exists in the data model but appears on only one screen is
worse than no field. You fill it in once and then never see it again, so you stop filling it in.
