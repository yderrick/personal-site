Twenty-five commits, two gates, v2.16.0 and v2.17.0.

Sharing was the big one. A share data model with public Firestore rules proven on the emulator before
anything touched production, payload chunking, and a sanitiser for the public surface. The reader
route mounts outside the app provider, so a reader loads none of the owner's app state. Reader
feedback lands in the same comments worklist I already had, rather than a parallel system nobody
would check.

Then series: a book can belong to a series, entities can live in a shared codex, and a share can
cover one book or the whole series. That meant a second subscription in the same listener pool, and
entity writes routed by location.

The other half of the day was help. A help-id registry, help content generated from the app's own
reference document rather than written twice, a HelpTip affordance with a popover, and a Help panel.
Generating it from the existing document means it can't drift from what the app actually does, which
is the only way documentation stays true.

Also renamed the thing. Worldbuilder, one word, canonically.
