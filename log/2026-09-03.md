Twenty-six commits, two gates, v2.32.0 and the AI settings work.

Dialogue attribution first. Working out who spoke a line used to rely on name co-occurrence — if a
name appeared near a quote, that was your speaker. Replaced it with a cascade of rules tried in
order, which is both more accurate and inspectable when it's wrong. Split a paragraph's quoted lines
into one utterance each, stopped dialogue-tag adjacency running past a sentence boundary, extended
the speech verbs, and surfaced where the pronoun rule can't tell gender rather than guessing. The
speaker word count now reads from the attribution engine instead of its own approximation.

Then bring-your-own-key AI. Provider adapters written to be CORS-safe from the browser, a settings
modal and storage, debounced and serialised writes, and error handling that believes the error body
over the status code when naming a failure — a 400 that says "invalid key" should say invalid key,
not "bad request".

Two details I liked: showing what each provider's key looks like, so you can tell whether you pasted
the right one, and defaulting Anthropic to Sonnet 5 rather than the cheapest model, because a wrong
answer costs more than the tokens saved.

Also gave power systems a reserve gauge — invocations have a wielder and a drain, reserves are
computed per character from the scenes that drain them, and there's a continuity check for
overdrawing a system. Removed the tier escalation ladder from the Systems Ledger; it was structure
without meaning.
