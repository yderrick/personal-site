I'm Derrick Yao Dzotefe. I run fuel and gas operations by day in Tema, Ghana, and build software the
rest of the time.

## The day job

I studied Petrochemical Engineering at KNUST, did my national service at BOST's Akosombo depot, and
since March 2024 I've been Stocks, Quality Control & Operations Team Lead — loading master — at
Quantum LPG Logistics. Custody transfer, stock reconciliation, quality checks, permits, and the
unglamorous discipline of making sure a terminal full of pressurised gas has an uneventful day.

Most of what I know about building things well, I learned from procedures that exist because someone
once skipped a step.

## The building

I'm not a career developer. I'm an engineer who noticed the barrier had moved and walked through it.
Everything I ship is built with Claude Code in VS Code, and I treat those projects the way I treat a
plant: written contracts up front, phased work, a changelog, release gates, and the failure path
tested before the happy path.

What's here:

- **Worldbuilder** — an app for fantasy writing and worldbuilding. A sixteen-category lore model,
  graph and map views, automatic linking between entries, offline-first with cloud sync.
- **This site** — Astro, deployed on every push, with a daily log of what I actually worked on rather
  than a highlight reel.
- **Daylog** — a single-user Android app that captures my day and makes it searchable, with every
  step running on the device rather than in someone else's cloud. Built in Kotlin, deliberately kept
  local.
- **Smaller things** — a job-search pipeline running on Google Apps Script and Sheets that reads my
  alert emails so I don't have to.

## The rest of it

I write — fantasy, worldbuilding, and poetry that keeps circling identity, fate, and whether anyone
gets to be forgiven. I also love the night sky, and I've been trying to photograph it with a phone
and no tripod, which is exactly as hard as it sounds.
